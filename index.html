MAKARIY LOMYKIN

I am a 16-year-old photographer based in Atlantic City, New Jersey, currently attending the Atlantic County Institute of Technology. Through my work, I explore how light, atmosphere, and architectural geometry form meaningful narratives within everyday environments.

My dedication to visual art has been recognized through three school Talon Awards for photography:

  • Best Black and White Photo: Echo
  • Best Color Photo: Where The Light Lingers
  • Best Nature Photo: Into The Deep

My ultimate ambition as an artist is to travel the world and capture perspectives that many may never have the opportunity to experience firsthand—especially those restricted by borders, citizenship limitations, or circumstance. Through photography, I strive to bridge these distances and share authentic, unfiltered glimpses of how I perceive the world.
