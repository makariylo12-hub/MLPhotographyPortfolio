01 — Where Light Finds Form

A study of light, structure, and natural form, exploring how ordinary environments can become visually compelling through perspective, composition, and timing.

02 — A Study in Color

An exploration of color as a visual language, using contrast, harmony, and atmosphere to shape emotion and guide the viewer through each image.

03 — Quiet Observations

A collection of moments often overlooked, focusing on the subtle details of people, places, and everyday environments that reveal something beyond the surface.

04 — Between the Seen and Unseen

An exploration of atmosphere, ambiguity, and emotion, using photography to capture moments that invite interpretation rather than provide a single answer.

MAKARIY LOMYKIN
